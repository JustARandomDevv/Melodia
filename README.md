# 🎵 Melodia: Your Ultimate Offline Music Companion

**Melodia** is a sleek, modern, and polished music application designed to give you complete freedom over your soundtrack. Browse, download, and build your ultimate offline music library with ease.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📥 High-Quality Downloads
* **YouTube Music Integration:** Download your favorite tracks in pristine, high-fidelity audio directly from YouTube Music.
* **True Offline Playback:** Need internet only to browse and download. Once saved, your music plays anywhere, anytime—no cellular data or Wi-Fi required.
* **Peer-to-Peer Sharing:** Since songs are saved directly to your device, you can instantly share your downloaded music files with friends.

### 🎧 Premium Listening Experience
* **Smart Playlist Maker:** Organize your soundscapes. Group tracks by mood, genre, or activity with an intuitive playlist manager.
* **Polished Interface:** Enjoy a clean, beautiful, and distraction-free UI designed around effortless navigation and smooth animations.
* **Rich Visuals:** Full music thumbnail and album art support to keep your library looking as vibrant as it sounds.
* **Intelligent Recommendations:** Discover your next favorite track with a personalized recommendation engine tailored to your listening habits.
* **Integrated Lyrics:** Sing along perfectly with built-in lyrics support (requires an active Wi-Fi/internet connection to load).

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1.  **Connect & Browse:** Connect to Wi-Fi and search for any song, artist, or album.
2.  **Download:** Tap the download icon next to your desired track to save it in high quality.
3.  **Go Offline:** Disconnect, head into nature, board a flight, or save your mobile data—your music travels with you!

---

## 🛠️ Tech & Requirements

* **Dependencies:** **FFmpeg** must be installed and configured on your system to handle high-quality audio conversion and processing. Ffmpeg.exe have to be in the same folder than the .exe
[**WINDOWS FFMPEG**](https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/packages/ffmpeg-2026-06-10-git-b29bdd3715-essentials_build.7z) ||||
[**LINUX (unbuntu) FFMPEG**](https://launchpad.net/ubuntu/+source/ffmpeg)
* **Network:** Wi-Fi/Data required for initial browsing, high-quality downloading, and fetching lyrics.
* **Storage:** Local device storage required for offline tracks and cached thumbnails.
